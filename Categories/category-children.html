Children's Health

Children are more vulnerable to certain infections because their immune systems are still developing. As a parent or caregiver, knowing the signs of common childhood conditions helps you act quickly and keep your child safe and healthy.

Chickenpox (Varicella)

Varicella-zoster virus, Itchy blister rash, fever, fatigue; preventable by vaccine

Measles (Rubeola)

Paramyxovirus, High fever, cough, Koplik's spots, full-body rash

Hand, Foot and Mouth Disease

Coxsackievirus, Mouth sores, rash on palms and soles, fever in young children

RSV (Respiratory Syncytial Virus)

Common respiratory virus, Runny nose, cough, wheezing; severe in infants

ADHD

Neurodevelopmental, Inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity affecting school performance

Kawasaki Disease

Inflammatory vasculitis, Prolonged fever, rash, swollen lymph nodes in children under 5
