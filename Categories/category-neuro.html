Neurological Diseases

The nervous system, brain, spinal cord, and nerves, controls everything we do. Neurological disorders affect millions worldwide and range from headaches to life-altering conditions like Parkinson's and Alzheimer's. Early diagnosis and specialist care are key to better outcomes.
