Acne Vulgaris

The most common skin condition worldwide, affecting up to 85% of people at some point in their lives. Acne occurs when hair follicles become clogged with oil, dead skin cells, and bacteria.

Symptoms

Acne affects approximately 650 million people globally. While commonly associated with teenage years, acne can occur at any age and can have significant effects on self-esteem and mental health.

  • Whiteheads, closed clogged pores
  • Blackheads, open clogged pores darkened by oxidation
  • Papules, small, tender red bumps
  • Pustules, pimples with pus at the tip
  • Nodules, large, solid, painful lumps deep in the skin
  • Cysts, painful, pus-filled lumps that can cause scarring
Causes

Acne develops when four main factors combine: excess oil production, clogged hair follicles, bacteria, and inflammation.

  • Propionibacterium acnes (C. acnes) bacteria overgrowth
  • Excess sebum production triggered by androgens
  • Hormonal changes, puberty, menstrual cycle, pregnancy
  • Certain medications, corticosteroids, lithium
  • High-glycaemic diet and dairy may worsen acne
  • Stress increases sebum production indirectly
Prevention
  • Wash face twice daily with gentle, non-comedogenic cleanser
  • Use oil-free, non-comedogenic moisturisers and sunscreen
  • Do not pick or squeeze pimples, causes scarring
  • Change pillowcases frequently
  • Keep hair clean and off face
  • Reduce high-glycaemic foods and dairy if they trigger breakouts
Treatment
  • Topical retinoids (Tretinoin, Adapalene), unclog pores
  • Benzoyl peroxide, kills acne bacteria
  • Topical or oral antibiotics for moderate acne
  • Oral contraceptives for hormonal acne in women
  • Isotretinoin (Accutane) for severe nodular acne
  • Chemical peels and laser therapy for scarring
