Generalized Anxiety Disorder

A mental health condition characterized by persistent, excessive, and uncontrollable worry about everyday situations, accompanied by physical symptoms like racing heart and muscle tension.

Symptoms

Anxiety disorders are the most common mental health conditions worldwide, affecting over 301 million people. They are highly treatable, yet most sufferers do not seek help due to stigma.

  • Excessive, uncontrollable worrying about multiple topics
  • Restlessness and feeling on edge
  • Rapid heartbeat, shortness of breath, chest tightness
  • Muscle tension, headaches, and fatigue
  • Difficulty concentrating, mind going blank
  • Sleep problems, difficulty falling or staying asleep
Causes

Anxiety disorders develop from a complex mix of genetic, neurological, environmental, and psychological factors.

  • Overactivity of the amygdala, the brain's fear center
  • Genetic predisposition, runs in families
  • Chronic stress and traumatic life events
  • Certain medical conditions (hyperthyroidism, heart arrhythmia)
  • Caffeine, stimulants, and withdrawal from alcohol
  • Personality traits, perfectionism and low distress tolerance
Prevention
  • Practice regular mindfulness and deep breathing exercises
  • Limit caffeine and alcohol consumption
  • Exercise regularly, releases natural anti-anxiety endorphins
  • Maintain consistent sleep routine
  • Seek therapy early, anxiety responds well to treatment
  • Build a strong social support network
Treatment
  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), most effective psychological treatment
  • SSRIs/SNRIs, first-line medications (Escitalopram, Sertraline)
  • Buspirone for GAD without sedation
  • short term benzodiazepines for acute anxiety, caution: habit-forming
  • Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR)
  • BetterHelp online therapy, accessible from home
