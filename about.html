About Us

We make health information clear, honest, and easy to understand.

Our mission

BHC Health Guide is an information website. We explain diseases, symptoms, causes, tests, treatments, and prevention in simple words so that you can understand what you read and ask better questions when you speak to a health professional.

How our guides are made

Every guide follows the same fixed outline and is checked by automated rules for structure and safety. Guides are based on widely published public health knowledge.

Our guides are not reviewed by doctors. We say this openly because you deserve to know. Read our editorial policy for the full process.

What we are not

We are not a clinic, a pharmacy, or a diagnosis tool. We cannot tell you what is wrong with you, and we never give medicine doses. If you are worried about your health, please see a qualified health professional.

Found a mistake?

Tell us through our contact page. We review every report and correct errors.
